# Project Idea

An Optional Monthly Contribution and Share Path for RaidGuild Members

### Project Submitted By

dekanbro / duckanbro is the human proposer, pilot administrator, and intended setup-payment recipient. The publishing agent acts as steward only.

## Summary

Propose an optional contribution pilot for existing members from **November 1, 2026 through January 1, 2027** (November and December). Nonpayment does not affect membership standing.

The proposed monthly cap is **200 wxDAI per member**, at **2.5 wxDAI per RG DAO share**, giving at most **80 shares per member per month**, or **160 across two months**. The earlier proposed minimum of 20 wxDAI remains for review. These are per-member limits, not an aggregate issuance cap or a limit on contribution-earned shares.

Members make manual wxDAI payments to a separately confirmed Guild contribution destination. The [Participation module](https://portal.raidguild.org/modules/portal-participation) verifies payments and prepares reports; it cannot mint shares. Each issuance batch requires independent human review and ordinary DAO approval. Reports should distinguish payment-based and contribution-earned shares and show their combined issuance.

## Why should we build this?

Test whether optional member contributions can support shared Guild infrastructure and provide another path to DAO ownership. Evaluate participation, infrastructure support, administrative effort, and combined issuance/dilution. The alternative is to retain the current contribution-earned share system without this pilot.
