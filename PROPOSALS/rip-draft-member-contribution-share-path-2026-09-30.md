# Project Idea

An Optional Monthly Contribution and Share Path for RaidGuild Members

### Project Submitted By

dekanbro / duckanbro is the human proposer, pilot administrator, and intended setup-payment recipient. The publishing agent acts as steward only.

## Summary

Propose an optional contribution pilot for existing members from **December 1, 2026 at 00:00 UTC through February 1, 2027 at 00:00 UTC** (December and January; end exclusive). Nonpayment does not affect membership standing.

The proposed monthly cap is **200 wxDAI per member**, at **2.5 wxDAI per RG DAO share**, giving at most **80 shares per member per month**, or **160 across two months**. The earlier proposed minimum of 20 wxDAI remains for review. These are per-member limits, not an aggregate issuance cap or a limit on contribution-earned shares.

Members make manual wxDAI payments to a separately confirmed Guild contribution destination. The [Participation module](https://portal.raidguild.org/modules/portal-participation) verifies payments and prepares reports; it cannot mint shares. Each issuance batch requires independent human review and ordinary DAO approval. Reports should distinguish payment-based and contribution-earned shares and show their combined issuance.

## Why should we build this?

Test whether optional member contributions can support shared Guild infrastructure and provide another path to DAO ownership. Evaluate participation, infrastructure support, administrative effort, and combined issuance/dilution. The alternative is to retain the current contribution-earned share system without this pilot.

## Resubmission history and unresolved feedback

[DAO proposal #228](https://admin.daohaus.club/molochv3/0x64/0xf02fd4286917270cb94fbc13a0f4e1ed76f7e986/proposal/228) was defeated: 400 Yes / 8,834 No, state 7, unprocessed on November 13, 2026. The new proposal would be a separate vote, not a replacement or cancellation of #228. It requests the same one-time setup payment, not an additional payment for work already funded by #228. No setup payment executed through that defeated, unprocessed proposal.

In [issue #237's formal discussion](https://github.com/raid-guild/RIPs/issues/237#issuecomment-6206077312), ECWireless questioned the rationale for 2.5 wxDAI per share and its perceived 50% discount, whether purchased shares stack with Participation Steward shares and allow investors to outpace active contributors, and the additional dilution. These concerns remain unresolved; the resubmission retains the proposed rate rather than treating the objection as settled.

In the [forum discussion](https://discord.com/channels/684227450204323876/1554878130207064134/1560468284770353264), ECWireless also questioned the funding need and intended change in member behavior, and raised the risk of investor dominance. The proposer described making ownership tangible and encouraging members to act as owners. ECWireless later expressed conditional interest in reinvestment around a new Guild chapter while questioning demand beyond existing large shareholders and evidence of smaller-shareholder interest. This is mixed feedback, not consensus.

[Additional October 23 feedback](https://discord.com/channels/684227450204323876/1554878130207064134/1563055547413176403) supported the core idea while questioning monetary share issuance versus participation incentives, the assumed $5/share comparison, and the appeal of a $20 monthly minimum and initial perks. It suggested an alternative $5/month cap with at most one share, with later tiers tied to features, and reported an abstention. That alternative is preserved for deliberation and is not adopted by this resubmission.

On [November 13](https://discord.com/channels/684227450204323876/1554875796869484724/1570821012965167184), dekanbro / duckanbro reported verifying that programmatic subdomain management is possible. This is the proposer's report, not independent technical verification by the steward. Perk costs, administration and access remain unresolved; this proposal does not authorize DNS changes or promise perks. The proposer described their own No vote as mostly accidental and also related it to wanting to verify subdomains. This does not explain or discount other voters' decisions.
