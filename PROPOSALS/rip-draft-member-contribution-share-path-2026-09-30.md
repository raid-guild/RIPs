# An Optional Monthly Contribution and Share Path for RaidGuild Members

Working ID: `rip-draft-member-contribution-share-path-2026-09-30`  
Status: discussion draft; no official RIP number assigned; not approved for execution.

### Project Submitted By

dekanbro / duckanbro is the human proposer, pilot administrator, and intended recipient of the requested one-time setup payment. The bot acts only as publisher and steward. The recipient's full payment address and the receiving steward's acceptance remain unconfirmed.

## Summary

Propose a two-month pilot of optional monthly contributions for existing RaidGuild members to support shared Guild infrastructure and offer a payment-based path to additional RG DAO shares. Participation is optional: nonpayment does not change membership standing.

The proposed monthly contribution range is 20–200 wxDAI per member. The earlier 20 wxDAI minimum remains for review. The proposer specified a monthly cap of 200 and a rate of 2.5 per share, equivalent to at most 80 RG DAO shares per member per month and 160 per member over two months. These are proposed terms, not a claim of DAO approval. They do not establish an aggregate issuance cap or impose a new cap on contribution-earned shares.

The proposer requested a two-month pilot ending around January 2027. November–December 2026, ending January 1, 2027, is one possible interpretation requiring confirmation. Exact dates remain unresolved.

## Why should we build this?

The pilot would test whether optional member contributions can support shared infrastructure while offering an additional path to DAO ownership. It must preserve the distinction between payment-based shares and shares earned for work under the Participation Steward framework. Both paths should be reported separately and together so the DAO can assess aggregate issuance and dilution.

The alternative is to make no change and retain the existing contribution-based share system.

## Proposed implementation

1. Confirm the pilot dates, payment destination, administrator responsibilities, independent reviewer, steward handoff, and operating rules before launch.
2. Members make manual wxDAI payments to the confirmed payment address. No payment address is specified by this draft.
3. The [Participation module](https://portal.raidguild.org/modules/portal-participation) verifies payments and prepares review reports. It cannot mint shares.
4. A human operator reconciles payments and prepares each batch. Another person independently reviews it.
5. The DAO decides on each issuance batch through its ordinary proposal process. Payment verification or passage of a pilot/setup proposal does not itself mint shares or guarantee that a later share proposal passes.
6. Reports distinguish payment-based issuance from contribution-earned Participation Steward issuance and show their combined total.

Monthly reconciliation is proposed; monthly versus two-month share-proposal cadence remains open. Refunds, mistaken payments, overpayments, and rejected share proposals need explicit rules before payments are collected.

## Governance boundary

Repository publication and submission of an exact DAO proposal each require the relevant approval. Merge, sponsorship, voting, processing, and future bonus payments are excluded from this preparation step.
